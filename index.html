Part A · About 6 minutes · No account needed

First look as a guest

  1. 1

    The welcome tour

    Do
    Open Flow for the first time.
    Expect
    A 7-step tour appears at the bottom. Each step switches to the screen it describes (Today, Projects, Focus, Insights). Back, Next and Skip work. On the last step, tap Load demo.
  2. 2

    Demo data fills the app

    Do
    After the tour, look at Today, then Projects, then Insights.
    Expect
    • Today: about 6 tasks grouped under Work, Personal and Family, with a day streak and XP at the top.
    • Projects: 4 projects. “Website Refresh” shows a red Overdue label.
    • Insights: a filled activity heatmap and a named work-style archetype.
  3. 3

    Complete, add and edit tasks

    Do
    Tap a task’s circle. Then tap Add a task to today, type a title, pick a priority and tap Add task. Then long-press any task.
    Expect
    The ticked task moves to the bottom of its group and the counts at the top update. The new task appears on Today. Long-press opens the editor, where you can change or delete the task.
  4. 4

    Projects

    Do
    On Projects, tap + and create a project. Then tap any project card.
    Expect
    The new project appears in the list. Tapping a card opens its editor, with Archive and Delete at the bottom.
  5. 5

    A focus session

    Do
    On Focus, press play. Switch to another app for about a minute, then come back. Tap the skip button to end the session.
    Expect
    The ring fills while running and the time is still correct after you return. After skipping, the screen says “Session complete”, and the session shows up in Insights → Weekly rhythm.
  6. 6

    Log water

    Do
    On Today, tap Log on the Hydration card.
    Expect
    The glass count goes up by one, and Insights → Health Garden shows the same number.
Part B · About 8 minutes · Uses your email

Account and sync

  1. 7

    Create an account

    Do
    Tap your avatar (top right on Today) → Settings → Create an account. Enter your name, an email you can open on this phone, and a password of at least 8 characters.
    Expect
    A message asks you to check your email.
  2. 8

    Confirm your email

    Do
    Open the confirmation email on the same phone and tap Confirm. If it hasn’t arrived, wait a few minutes before asking for another.
    Expect
    Flow opens and you’re signed in. If it opens a browser page instead, go back to Flow and sign in with your email and password.
  3. 9

    Your data moves into your account

    Do
    Look at the avatar and open Settings.
    Expect
    The avatar shows your initials. The Settings card shows your name and email, and within a few seconds says All changes synced.
  4. 10

    Changes sync on their own

    Do
    Add a task, wait about 5 seconds, then open Settings.
    Expect
    Settings briefly shows “Syncing…”, then All changes synced.
  5. 11

    Works offline

    Do
    Turn on airplane mode and complete a task. Open Settings. Then turn airplane mode off and wait a few seconds.
    Expect
    While offline, the task still ticks and Settings shows 1 change waiting. After reconnecting, it changes to All changes synced.
  6. 12

    Sign out and back in

    Do
    Settings → Sign Out. Then Settings → Sign in to sync with the same email and password.
    Expect
    After signing out you stay in the app as a guest and your tasks are cleared from the phone. After signing in again, your tasks come back from the cloud within a few seconds.
Part C · About 4 minutes

Settings and extras

  1. 13

    Biometric lock

    Do
    Settings → turn on Biometric lock and confirm with your fingerprint, face or PIN. Leave Flow for at least 30 seconds, then reopen it.
    Expect
    Flow shows “Flow is locked” and asks you to unlock before showing your data.
  2. 14

    Export your data

    Do
    Settings → Export my data.
    Expect
    The phone’s share sheet opens with your data as text. You can save it or cancel.
  3. 15

    Change your name

    Do
    Settings → Profile, change your name, tap Save.
    Expect
    The avatar initials and the Settings card update to the new name.
  4. 16

    Reminders and the tour replay

    Do
    Settings → turn on Focus reminder and Daily Spark, allowing notifications when asked. Then Settings → Take the tour.
    Expect
    Both switches stay on (reminders arrive daily at 09:30 and 08:00). The tour starts again from the welcome step.
Good to know

Not bugs

First launch is empty. A new install has no tasks until you add some or load the demo data.

Emails can take a few minutes. During testing, only a few sign-up emails can be sent per hour.

Step counts aren’t synced yet. The Health Garden shows water you log. Automatic step counting is planned.

Friends leaderboard is coming. Insights shows your own weekly XP for now.
